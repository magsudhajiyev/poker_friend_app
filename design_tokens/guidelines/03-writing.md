# Writing

Final Table talks like a sharp friend at the table: brief, confident, a little dry. The site's line sets the tone — *"Log a hand in three gestures, not three minutes."*

## Voice

- Address the player as **you**. Short words, short sentences. No exclamation marks or emoji.
- Speak poker natively (see Poker conventions); never explain "BTN" or "3-bet".
- No judgement of play or results. "−450" is a fact, not "Tough session!".

## Case

The file mixes case today ("Recent Activity", "Edit Bankroll" next to "Log your first session", "View all"). The rule from now on:

- **Title Case** for section headers, tab labels and screen titles — they act as names: *Recent Activity*, *Recent Sessions*, *Earnings*, *Total Bankroll*.
- **Sentence case** for everything that reads as a sentence or an action: buttons, banners, empty states, helper text — *Edit bankroll*, *View all*, *Log your first session*.

## Patterns

| Situation | Write | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empty-state banner | Log your first session · Your stats will show up here once you start tracking | Nothing here yet :( |
| No goal set | No goal set yet | Goal: none |
| Goal progress | $55K left · 72% | 72% complete! |
| Undo | Hand deleted · Undo | Are you sure you want to delete? |
| Offline | Offline · 3 hands waiting to sync | Error: no connection |
| Error | Couldn't sync. Retrying when you're back online. | Something went wrong |
