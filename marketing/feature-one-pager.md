## The one-liner

**Final Table tracks every hand you play — not just your profit at the end of the night.**

## The pitch (30 seconds)

Most poker apps tell you how much you won. Final Table tells you *why*. Log actions street by street and watch your real stats build from your actual play: VPIP, PFR, 3-bet, c-bet, steal, check-raise, fold-to-3-bet — broken down by position. After enough hands, the app tells you what kind of player you are: TAG, LAG, Nit, Calling Station, Maniac, or Balanced. Then it helps you fix it: AI hand review in your language at your level, GTO training drills, and your personal 13×13 range grid.

## Feature pillars

### 1. Log hands your way
- **Full Hand Logging** — every action, every street, every player. Side pots, chopped pots, all-ins, and dead-button scenarios handled automatically. Undo anything.
- **Quick Session** — just buy-in, cash-out, done. Cash and tournaments.
- **Dealer Voice Logging** — a dealer runs the table hands-free with voice recognition while every player follows live from their own phone.

### 2. Know your real game
- 7 pro-level stats calculated from your actual play, by position
- Play-style classification after 50 hands (TAG / LAG / Nit / Calling Station / Maniac / Balanced)
- Personal 13×13 range grid — see which hands you *actually* play vs. how you think you play
- Hand replay and per-session analytics with stack-growth charts

### 3. Improve on purpose
- **AI hand review** — street-by-street analysis, written in your app's language and matched to your experience level. Saved forever.
- **Training drills** — open-raise, 3-bet, and blind-defense charts, plus Nash push-fold practice against bots
- **Export everything** — PokerStars-format hand histories, CSV, or share a single hand as a link anyone can open

### 4. Run real games
- Create a club, invite players and dealers, host multi-table cash games and tournaments
- QR codes handle seat assignments; live rankings update in real time on every phone
- Full-screen leaderboard display for the venue TV
- **One subscription runs the whole club** — the owner pays, every member, dealer, and player uses the app free

### 5. Built for real players
- Tournament tracking: buy-ins, rebuys, add-ons, ITM%, ROI, average finish, final tables, hourly rate
- Bankroll goals with a pinch-to-zoom earnings chart, cash and tournament split out
- Private opponent book: profiles, notes, and playing-style tags for the regulars in your game
- Shareable post-session summary card (duration, net P/L, biggest pot, best/worst hand)
- Works offline — everything saves locally and syncs when you're back
- 11 languages: EN, DE, ES, FR, IT, PL, PT, RO, RU, SK, UK (poker terms stay in English)
