# Final Table — 40-Second Social Video Scripts (2-person home-office cut)

*Created 2026-09-06, simplified same day for a TWO-PERSON crew in one
home-office room. From `MARKETING_MASTER.md`; compliance-checked against
GUARDRAILS: no trial claims, no opponent stat-tracking claims, no iOS, no
winning promises, no unreleased features. CTA is always Google Play.*

**The whole kit:** desk + chair, desk lamp (it's also the "dramatic
lighting"), phone tripod, second phone for screen recordings, a deck of
cards, 2–3 chip stacks, a hoodie + a cap (character changes), monitor or
TV in shot for one script. That's everything.

**Production rules (apply to all):**
- 9:16 vertical; every video works MUTED — burn in captions.
- Screen recordings do the heavy lifting: seeded account (100+ hands),
  English locale, release build, never debug menus or real user data.
- One actor can be two characters: jump cut + cap/hoodie swap. Own it,
  it reads as a bit, not a budget.
- End card (last 3s, identical every time): app icon + "**Final Table**
  — free on Google Play" + store badge.
- The first 2 seconds decide everything — shoot hooks with double takes.

## 2. "The Dealer Just Talks" — voice logging
*Persona: host · Feature: dealer voice · Cast: A deals, B films/reacts*

**Sound-on video. The desk corner is the "table" — throw a dark cloth or
hoodie flat on it, deal onto that. Frame TIGHT: hands, cards, phone.**

| Time | Visual | Audio / VO | Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Tight on A's mouth + hands dealing: "Seat three raises to twelve." | dealer voice, crisp | "watch the phone 👇" |
| 2–12s | Split screen: hands dealing on desk + phone screen. Actions appear AS SPOKEN: "seat five calls… flop ace of spades, king of hearts, queen of diamonds." | live audio | live subtitles |
| 12–20s | Second phone (B's) propped against a mug: same hand, live. Camera racks focus between the two phones. | ambient | "every player sees it live" |
| 20–28s | B's hand reaches in, taps replay, the hand replays on screen. | music | "no typing. all night. logged." |
| 28–34s | A shuffles, grins at camera: "I just… talk." | dialogue | "dealer talks. app does the rest." |
| 34–40s | End card. | VO: "Your home game just went pro." | CTA |

**Notes:** rehearse the exact phrases against the parser FIRST — quiet
room, phone 30–50cm away. Tight framing means nobody can tell it's a
desk and not a poker table. Highest effort, highest ceiling.

## Shooting-day plan (one afternoon, one room)

1. **Block 1 — screen captures first** (no actors needed): stats scroll,
   play-style reveal, range grid + filters, tournament stats, quick
   session, hand replay, summary card, TV leaderboard. One clean batch
   feeds scripts 1, 3, 4, 5, 6.
2. **Block 2 — talking heads & bits**: script 3 (solo, easiest), then 1,
   then 5's costume jump cuts (funniest when you're already loose).
3. **Block 3 — the dealer video** (2): needs quiet + parser rehearsal;
   do it last with the room settled.

## Platform cheatsheet

- Native uploads per platform (no cross-watermarks). Cold-audience order:
  **1 → 4 → 6 → 3 → 5 → 2** — the dealer video lands once there's an
  audience to wow.
- Captions burned into every spoken line; script 2 keeps sound as the
  punchline but is captioned anyway.
- Hashtag base: #poker #homegame #pokertok + local-language tags (the 11
  localized listings are live — RO/PL/DE caption variants are cheap wins).

  their stats".
- ❌ No iOS/App Store mention — Google Play only.

  teaches.
